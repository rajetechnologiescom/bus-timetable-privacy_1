Privacy Policy

Bus Timetable · Last updated: 15 September 2026

This policy explains what information the Bus Timetable mobile application (“the app”) collects, why it is collected, and how it is handled.

The app is provided by Raje Tech (“we”, “us”). For any question about this policy or your data, contact us at rajetechnologies.com@gmail.com.

Summary

The app does not require an account and does not ask you to register. You can search bus routes and view timetables without providing any personal information. Information is only sent off your device in two situations: when you choose to submit feedback or report an issue, and when advertisements are displayed.

Information we collect

1. Feedback and issue reports (optional)

If you choose to use the feedback form or the “report an issue” feature, we receive the information you submit. This may include:

  • a rating and any comment you write
  • the route identifier and route name the report relates to
  • the type of issue selected
  • the date and time of submission, the app version, and the status of the report

The comment fields are free text. We do not ask for your name, email address, or telephone number, and we ask that you do not include them. If you choose to enter personal details in a comment, that information is stored with your submission.

This data is submitted only when you actively send it. If you never use these features, nothing is sent.

2. Advertising information

The app displays advertisements supplied by Google AdMob. To serve and measure advertisements, the AdMob software development kit collects certain information from your device, including your Android advertising identifier and information about your interaction with advertisements (such as when an advertisement is displayed or tapped). This information is collected by Google, not by us, and is transmitted to Google as an independent third party.

For details of how Google uses this information, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. You can reset or delete your advertising identifier, and limit ad personalisation, in your Android device settings under Settings → Privacy → Ads.

3. Information stored only on your device

The app saves the following to your device's local storage so that it is quicker to use:

  • your recently selected routes (route, origin, destination, time, service type and depot)
  • a cached list of station names

This information stays on your device. It is not transmitted to us or to anyone else, and it is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

Information we do not collect

The app does not collect your precise or approximate location, your contacts, your photos, videos, audio, files, calendar, messages, health information, or any payment or financial information. The app requests no location permission.

Why we use this information

InformationPurpose
Feedback and issue reports To read and act on the problems you report, correct timetable or route information, and improve the app
Advertising identifier and ad interactions To display advertisements, measure their performance, and detect invalid or fraudulent ad traffic. Collected and used by Google
Locally stored searches and station cache To show your recent routes and load the app faster. Never leaves your device

Who has access to your information

Feedback and issue reports are stored in Google Cloud Firestore (Firebase), a hosting service that stores this data on our behalf and under our instructions. We do not sell this information, and we do not share it with any other company or organisation.

Advertising information is collected by and shared with Google as the operator of the AdMob advertising network.

We may disclose information if required to do so by law, or to establish, exercise, or defend a legal claim.

How long we keep your information

Feedback and issue reports are kept for as long as they are useful for correcting and improving the service, and are deleted when no longer required. Advertising information is retained by Google in accordance with its own retention policies.

Security

All information sent from the app travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Stored data is held in Google Cloud infrastructure with access restricted to us.

Your rights and data deletion

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request access to the personal information we hold about you, to have it corrected, or to have it deleted. Raje Tech is based in India, and requests from users in India are handled in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. The email address below is also our contact point for any grievance relating to the handling of your personal data.

To make a request, email rajetechnologies.com@gmail.com. Because feedback and reports are submitted without an account, we cannot identify which submission is yours from an email address alone. Please include the approximate date of your submission and the route it related to, so that we can locate it. We will respond within 30 days.

Data held only on your device can be removed at any time by clearing the app's storage (Settings → Apps → Bus Timetable → Storage → Clear data) or by uninstalling the app.

Children

The app is a general-audience travel tool. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted personal information through the feedback form, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be published on this page with an updated date at the top. Significant changes will be reflected in the app's Google Play listing.
